I studied **Applied Mathematics at Amirkabir University of Technology** (2012–2017), where the courses that stuck were the ones about structure: theory of computation, algorithms, graphs, linear algebra, probability.

Since 2020 I've worked as a data scientist, ML engineer, technical lead and co-founder — classifying images for a marketplace, forecasting a mall's electricity, making a bank's ML services ship ten times faster, leading a team that built a RAG assistant for aircraft maintenance, and teaching social agents to call a language model only when they truly need one.

What ties it together is a belief that *most problems get simpler when you find the right mathematical object to hold them* — a distribution, a graph, a network of beliefs.

Away from work I read philosophy and cognitive science, sketch geometry, learn Rust, and I translated *Mindfulness for Teens with ADHD* into Persian at the request of Dr. Mandegar Nikoo.
